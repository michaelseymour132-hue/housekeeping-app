Housekeeping Hub

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

Housekeeping Hub ("the service") is provided by the operator of Housekeeping Hub ("we", "us", "our"). Businesses (hotels, guest houses and similar) use it to run their housekeeping. For any privacy question or request, contact us at the address shown in the app.

Who is responsible for what

Each business that uses Housekeeping Hub decides what its team records and is responsible for that information. We store and protect it on the business's behalf and only use it to provide the service. For your account details as a customer, we are responsible.

What we store

The information needed to run housekeeping: staff names and email addresses; roles and property assignments; records of cleaning, inspection and deep-clean activity (including times and who did them); room notes and maintenance reports; photographs staff upload of rooms, issues and deep cleans; documents a business uploads; and, if a business imports them, guest names and stay dates used to plan cleaning.

What we do not do

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal data.

Lawful basis

We process this information to provide the service a business has signed up for (contract) and to operate, secure and improve it (legitimate interests). Businesses process their staff's information as part of the working relationship.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while a business uses the service. If a business stops using it, its records are deleted within 12 months unless the business asks us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer. Deactivated staff profiles are kept, marked inactive, so the history of work stays accurate.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may have the right to access, correct, export or ask for deletion of your personal information. Administrators can export their data from Settings at any time. If you work for a business that uses Housekeeping Hub, ask your manager first; you can also contact us. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Every account needs a sign-in, handled by Firebase Authentication, and all traffic is encrypted. Each business's information is kept separate and access is limited by role. Every change is recorded in an audit log.

Who helps us run the service

Google Firebase stores the information and handles sign-in and file storage. Vercel serves the website. Both process information only on our instructions, under their data-processing terms.

Contact

Questions or requests: the address shown in the app.
