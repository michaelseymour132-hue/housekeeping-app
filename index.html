Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. Sign up with your email and use everything free for the whole trial.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged. Your rooms, history and photos stay safe and you can still view them. Choose a plan to switch changes back on for your team.

Does my team need to download anything?

No. It runs in the web browser on any phone, tablet or computer, and can be added to the home screen so it opens like an app.

Do I need booking or property management software?

No. Housekeeping Hub works on its own. You can import arrivals and departures from a spreadsheet, and automatic calendar sync is on the way.

Is my data safe?

Each business's data is kept completely separate and is stored with Google's Firebase cloud service. Staff only see what their role allows, and every change is logged. You can export your data any time.

How many staff can I add?

As many as you need - every plan includes unlimited staff.
